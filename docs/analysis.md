Two rules for how you report every number.

First, no percentage travels without its error. requires_study_partner on the value required is 100 percent precision and 83 percent recall against forty hand labelled trials. That sentence goes next to your headline number, not in a footnote at the end. A percentage produced by a machine, reported without saying how accurate the machine is, is not a finding.

Second, small samples have wide error bars and there are two different numbers here. A single band’s percentage, near 30 percent with 150 trials, is accurate to about 7 points either way. But comparing two bands stacks both errors, so a difference between bands has to exceed about 11 points before it means anything. If the study partner requirement goes from 24 percent in one band to 31 percent in the next, your honest sentence is that you did not detect a change, not that it rose by seven points.
