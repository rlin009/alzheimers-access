# Router test: three people who have never seen Free the Burp

Step 10. Do this with three real people. Fill it in after each one.

## How to run it

1. Open https://alzheimers-access.vercel.app/free-the-burp on their phone or laptop.
2. Say this out loud, and nothing else: "Find out what might explain food getting stuck."
3. Then stay quiet. Do not explain, point or rescue them when they pause.
4. Write down every place they hesitate, scroll back, or tap the wrong thing.
5. When they say they are done, ask one question: "What would you ask a doctor for now?"

If two people get stuck in the same place, that is the site's fault, not theirs. Write it in the last section.
